A plan can be a working artifact rather than a record: kept local, excluded from git, deleted with the worktree when the work closes, so that no second explanation of the change ships and drifts. The method should allow that form and give it its rule: before the plan's folder is removed, each kind of content moves to the record that survives, by a table (the why of the change to the change log, what was delivered to the ticket, how it was verified to the pull request, anything deferred to a new tracker item, a lasting decision to the decisions log), and the `close` skill reads rulings from wherever the plan kept them without assuming the plan persists.

## Evidence

Several carriers of one team keep their plans in an ignored folder by a written rule, with exactly such a table; the method's `close` skill copies rulings from the plan's ledger and was found to conflict with that rule in one carrier's execution review (meta/reviews/2026-10-07-host-ticket-cycle.md).
