This replaces the catalogue's trigger and offer in `p-480f968069`, after research the same day. A single complexity signal routes straight to its skill: an unknown failure cause to systematic debugging, independent parts to parallel dispatch. The role catalogue is read only on two or more signals, and again when a plan is written or the work passes its third committable step. At most three skills are offered, a line each with why it fits, the skipped ones named in one line, and the human is asked only when the choice is not reversible; otherwise the agent states it and proceeds. Each catalogue row says what separates it from its neighbours (executing a plan, driving subagents and parallel dispatch; a code review request and the bootstrap's fresh-context review; `close` and finishing a branch). One sentence says the method's order wins over a skill description that demands to run first. Each carrier's `LOCAL` file names the roles it has installed, and a check fails when a listed skill is missing. The bundle also advises each carrier to trim its skill listing, turning off or showing by name only the skills that do not fit it, toward a couple of dozen without near twins, since the host drops descriptions past a share of the context, least used first.

## Evidence

Decisions d-5ed7e8-0372b4 and d-5ed7e8-4c80c1, by the owner, superseding d-5ed7e8-2ec5dd and d-5ed7e8-18ff9c. Research in `meta/reviews/2026-10-07-skill-selection-at-scale.md`: selection accuracy falls past a few dozen skills and near-twin competitors cause most wrong picks; offering two or three gains more than four or more; any one of five loose signals fires on most ordinary work; the host's listing drops descriptions on a machine with over a hundred skills. The trigger eval will measure a trimmed listing against the current one.
