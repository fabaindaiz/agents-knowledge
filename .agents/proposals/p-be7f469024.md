The adoption table's row for settled decisions changes: a host that keeps ADR files keeps them; the method asks only that their statuses map to its vocabulary (`rejected` to `declined`, `pending` to `proposed`), that an index row per record carries Enforced in, and that supersession is written both ways; the mapping is recorded in `adapted`, and the decisions check reads the host's index.

## Evidence

Principle 19: the host's shapes win and the guarantees do not bend. The ADR templates in common use name their states differently (Nygard and MADR use rejected; Tyree and Akerman use pending, decided, approved), so a status check needs a recorded mapping to read them.
