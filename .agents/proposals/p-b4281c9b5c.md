A meta-session lists the carriers on its machine by searching for bundle folders, and compares what it finds with the local manifest, rather than trusting the manifest: a carrier adopted in a session that did not write the manifest is otherwise never reached, never registered, and never gathered. Stops applying where adoption itself writes the manifest. Costs one filesystem search.

## Evidence

The manifest of one machine listed three carriers; a search for bundle folders found eight, three of them adopted in the preceding days and absent from the registry, one holding four proposals nobody had gathered. Literature: none consulted.
