The decisions log gains a section, *Looks deliberate, is not* (`| Id | What | Why it is not deliberate | Fix when |`, a `d-` id per row, deleted when fixed), which tells an agent what it may fix without asking: principle 10 inverted. Artifact 6 gains short criteria for writing a row: a change that adds or removes a runtime dependency, moves a module boundary, changes persisted data, changes a public interface, is hard to revert, is a choice a reviewer would question (not doing the common thing included), or fixes a cross-cutting convention; not a fix, a rename, a lint, or what an accepted row already covers. The criteria stay out of the coding session.

## Evidence

Two carriers already keep declared gaps and visible debt beside their decisions. Carriers' logs differ in size by more than an order of magnitude, so they share no threshold for what deserves a row. An external team's practice keeps a known-debt list and concrete criteria. The coding session has about two hundred tokens of budget left, and the criteria are rung 1 wherever they are written, since no check can judge them.
