# Proposal: a-finding-is-written-while-it-is-found

A finding is written while it is found, candidly, in the form a published study would use: what was run, the
number as it came out, what it refutes including the writer's own earlier claim, and what remains unexplained —
rather than a summary composed at the end, when the inconvenient half has already been smoothed away. The
writing is incremental because the record of a measurement is part of the measurement: a figure recomputed from
the data at the end of a long session is a figure nobody can check against what happened. Where a claim is
inferred rather than measured, it says so in the text and not only in a header.

## Evidence

Stated by one human as a standing instruction for an experiment programme, and held for weeks in an assistant's
local memory on one machine rather than in the repository that the programme lives in — so on any other machine
the instruction does not exist. Applied in that repository's own research documents, which carry per-finding
marks for what was read at the source and what is a bibliographic record only, and in a 2026-10-15 release whose
three research documents and closing review each name what they could not verify. No measurement: what would
settle it is whether a reader of those documents can tell a measured figure from an inferred one without asking,
counted over a set of them. Literature: none consulted; the practice is ordinary scientific reporting.
