A gate whose output is piped through a filter before the next step reports the filter's exit status, not the gate's: a check piped into a text filter and chained to a commit commits after a failing check. A gate that must block runs on its own, or under `set -o pipefail`, and its status is read before anything that depends on it. Stops applying where the pipeline's status is taken with pipefail. Costs nothing but the habit.

## Evidence

In one release session the home's check printed a failure through a filter and the chained commit and push went ahead; the release folder was pushed behind its sources and had to be restored by a second commit. The repository's own pre-commit hook checked privacy only, so nothing else stopped it. Literature: POSIX shell pipeline semantics (the exit status of a pipeline is that of its last command) and the pipefail option.
