Before a meta-session gathers a carrier, it compares the carrier's branch with its remote, because the gather reads the working tree: a checkout left behind by a history rewrite, or simply never pulled, offers an old bundle and old learnings as if they were current. Where the two differ, the remote is the record unless the user says otherwise, and the local branch is kept under a backup name before it is moved. Stops applying to a carrier with no remote, whose checkout is the record. Costs one fetch and one comparison per carrier.

## Evidence

In one meta-session, three of eight carriers had local branches that shared no recent history with their remotes after a rewrite of commit trailers; one of them held a bundle two releases older than its remote. The first read of the carriers reported the old versions; only a comparison with each upstream showed the difference. Literature: none consulted; it is ordinary source-control hygiene applied to a tool that reads the working tree.
