Gathering reads a carrier's working tree, so a carrier whose checkout is held by another live session (on another branch) offers its old proposals and hides the ones its harvest committed on a branch. A pack written from a temporary worktree of the harvest branch carried them; the procedure should name that path, or the gather tool should accept a branch.

## Evidence

Two carriers in one meta-session had live sessions on other branches; the first gather saw the older proposal count for one of them and none of the new ones. A temporary worktree of the harvest branch, packed and passed with `--packs`, closed the gap; the worktree was removed after.
