A message to the human that cites record ids (roadmap items, decisions, proposals, session entries) carries, in the same message, a table with at least each id's name and, where it matters, its state. A bare id tells a reader nothing who cannot look it up at that moment, and a person following a session remotely usually cannot.

## Evidence

The owner of one carrier asked for it as a requirement after a status report cited about a dozen ids with no names, on 2026-10-08; every report after it in that session carried the table, and the owner's questions turned from "what is this" to the decisions themselves. One carrier's evidence; it belongs beside §15's review turn, which already fixes the shape of a question.
