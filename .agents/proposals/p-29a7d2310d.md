# Proposal: a-retry-loop-needs-a-ceiling-and-a-dead-letter

Retries multiply across layers: three retries at each of five layers turn one failing call into about two hundred and forty at the failing dependency. Retry at one layer only, with a per-process budget and jitter, never on a permanent error or an unhealthy dependency. The check: inject failures into the dependency and watch whether its load rises more than about twice.

## Evidence

AWS Builders' Library, *Timeouts, retries, and backoff with jitter* (2019); Google SRE book, *Addressing Cascading Failures* (a retry budget per process).
