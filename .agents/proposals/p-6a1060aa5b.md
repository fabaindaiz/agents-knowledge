When an assistant consults the knowledge base for a design answer, picking cards by their titles reaches a fraction of the ones that bear on the question; following the knowledge base's own routing, its read-me and then its index, reaches several times as many, and what the extra cards add is mostly failure handling that a design answer otherwise leaves out.

## Evidence

A pilot in a repository started from a template, before its bootstrap: one open system-design question spanning several components and an effect that cannot be undone, answered by the same model under different conditions. Picking by title from a directory listing read 7 cards. Two fresh agents told to follow the read-me and the index read about twenty cards each, plus a few full notes. What only the routed answers held were concerns existing notes name: an invariant enforced in the store rather than in process memory, a missing signal refusing rather than reading as zero, an out-of-band check of a model's output, a baseline against a trivial predictor, per-component readout, a kill switch on every path, no simultaneous deploy, best-effort side channels, and a testing section. The repository had no root instruction file yet, so nothing told the session holding the conversation to route through the index.
