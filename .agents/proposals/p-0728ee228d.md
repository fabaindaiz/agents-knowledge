`bundle.py new entry` and `count` default to `.claude/logs/agent-changelog.md`, and `carrier.toml` has no key for a carrier's own log path, so every carrier that keeps its log elsewhere passes `--log` by hand each time, and a log whose older entries use another heading level receives its first new entry at the format block. A `log` key in `carrier.toml` would fix the default.

## Evidence

Four carriers updated or bootstrapped in one session keep their log under another assistant's folder; each needed the path given by hand, and one first entry landed at the bottom of its log.
