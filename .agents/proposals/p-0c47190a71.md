`release.py register` and `align`, given a temporary worktree of a carrier's branch (the only way to reach a carrier whose checkout another live session holds), record the worktree's scratch path in the machine's manifest as that carrier's path, and a list entry for it; the next read-only command would then look for a folder that no longer exists. They should resolve a linked worktree to its main repository's path, or refuse a path outside it.

## Evidence

In one meta-session two carriers with live sessions were registered and aligned through scratch worktrees of their harvest branches; the manifest gained two scratch paths and two duplicate records, removed by hand after the worktrees were deleted.
