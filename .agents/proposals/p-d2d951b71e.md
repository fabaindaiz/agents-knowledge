an experiment that copies a live artefact into each trial's workspace measures whatever the artefact is at that moment; each trial records the digest of what it received, and the run refuses a trial whose digest is not the plan's. Stops applying when the treatment is copied once, before the run. Costs one hash per trial

## Evidence

An evaluation run of about two dozen trials copied a live instruction bundle per trial; two fix rounds rebuilt it mid-run, and a quarter of one arm's non-trivial trials ran a later version, found only afterwards by comparing sizes. Literature: preregistration and treatment-fidelity practice in trials (the delivered treatment is recorded, not assumed)
