A template's empty default is absence, not a decision: where one record of a repository's own fields still holds the template's empty list and another holds its real list, the two do not disagree, and settling them by picking one as a peer value can erase every recorded decision. Keep "never filled" distinct from "deliberately empty" up to the step that merges them.

## Evidence

Four carriers on an old layout each held their own fields in several headers; one header kept the template's empty lists while the others held between seven and fourteen recorded adaptations and refusals. The conversion refused them as disagreeing, and a review warned that settling on the empty lists would make the next update re-propose everything those carriers had declined. The full lists were carried. Literature: the note's own.
