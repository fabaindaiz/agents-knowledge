# Proposal: fresh-context-review-before-a-cut

A release that its author believes finished is not finished. Reviewers in a fresh context, each given a disjoint half of the release (the code in one, the prose and records in the other) and a cheaper model than the session, found defects of kinds the author's own gate does not see: a check that never fired in its default invocation, an allowance able to hide a second mistake, a pattern that could turn a close red on a cited id, a security instruction that would let a suspect tool clear itself, a field written and never read, a note citing literature it had not read, the same evidence counted in three notes, and queue rows shipped without a history line. Splitting by disjoint halves keeps each brief small and the findings uncorrelated with the author's reading. The method already asks for review at a cut; this adds the split and the cheaper model as the default shape, and the defect classes as what to brief for.

## Evidence

One release, one session, two delegates on a cheaper model than the session, decided beforehand by a recorded decision (`d-5ed7e8-d9ebfb`: one over the tools, one over the method's prose). The defects listed above are those the session reported finding between them. Earlier measurements of reviews in a fresh context in this bundle (three reviews, six, seven and three important findings, no critical one) are recorded in the history for `isolated-review-by-default`; this release adds a fourth batch to them.
