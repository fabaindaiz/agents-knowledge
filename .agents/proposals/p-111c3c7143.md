A step that converts a source into another form and then removes the source must refuse, removing nothing, when the source holds anything it did not read: a reader that recognises only the shape it expects turns every other shape into silence, and the removal makes the silence permanent. Stops applying when the source is kept, or the conversion is checked against it before the removal. Costs one pass that lists what was not read.

## Evidence

An adversarial review of a release tool reproduced it: a table read only when its header matched to the space was converted into zero records, then deleted; a formatter's aligned columns, or rows after a blank line, were enough. Fixed by reading the header by its cells and refusing when any line is neither a row nor the known surrounding text. Literature: the note's own.
