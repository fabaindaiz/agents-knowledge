Rows a carrier added over its release that are neither candidates nor runs, such as queued experiments, are converted by the splice into proposals like the rest; until the tool does that, the home holds such rows where no build reads them, never in a tracking file that generates a shipped page. Taking them into the experiments queue between releases changes the shipped open-questions page and leaves the release folder behind its sources.

## Evidence

A carrier on an old release had added five queued experiments beside fifteen candidate rows; the splice turned the candidates into proposals but refused the experiments as lines it would remove unread. Taking them into the home's experiments queue let the splice run, and silently made the shipped open-questions page stale against its sources, found by the home's check after the commit. They were moved to the roadmap until the next release. Literature: none.
