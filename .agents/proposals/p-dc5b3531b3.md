# Proposal: prompt-harvest

A multi-carrier harvest designs its plan faster when its scratch has fixed reference sections, set before any reading starts, that every delegated reader fills the same way and the consolidation reads in one order. Per carrier, five: (a) what changed since the frontier, from the records; (b) what the human said, from the transcripts, with corrections and free-text answers marked; (c) frictions counted by searching the log, never from memory; (d) candidate learnings, each with its target slug and whether it extends, overlaps or is new; (e) what belongs to that repository only, for its own roadmap. Consolidated, four piles with a recommendation each, plus one apart: A, tool and method defects with a mechanism; B, extensions of a note or candidate with a new number, boundary or occurrence; C, new single-occurrence learnings marked thin; D, recommended drops (textbook, or a restatement of the owner's own instruction file); and *for the owner now* — what is urgent and not the bundle's, such as a credential seen in a transcript, named by kind only. The common brief every reader gets is written once and pasted, so the sections arrive comparable and the owner approves one table instead of reconciling a dozen formats.

## Evidence

A harvest over about fifteen carriers of one owner and the home, with one delegated reader per group of carriers: the first readers were briefed in prose and returned a different structure each, and reconciling them took longer than the reading. Once the five per-carrier sections and the four piles were fixed and pasted into every brief, the later reports dropped into the consolidated list with no reshaping, and the owner approved the whole table by row numbers in one answer. The *for the owner now* section exists because the readers found live credentials in transcripts: kept apart, they were reported to the owner by kind and never entered a proposal or the scratch list. One harvest, one owner; a description of the format and what it removed, not a measured comparison. The method's harvest prompt names what to read and how to propose, and is silent on the shape of the working notes between those two steps, which is where a large harvest spends its time.
