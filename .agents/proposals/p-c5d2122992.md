# Proposal: record-the-estimate-and-actual-of-a-delegation

An estimate given before the first delegation of a kind is a guess, and it can be wrong by an order of magnitude. What makes the next estimate useful is the pair, estimate and actual, written down at the time. The method already announces time, tokens and model before a long agent runs and compares them on return; it should add that the pair is kept in the repository beside the work it paid for, and that an estimate with no recorded pair for that kind of task is labelled as a guess. Without the record, the miss is felt and forgotten, and the next announcement repeats it.

## Evidence

One session. Three research agents on a cheaper model returned at about 69k, 55k and 19k tokens, taking roughly one to two minutes each, as written in the headers of their three research documents. The session had announced a far larger figure for each before launching them; that announcement was spoken, not written, so the pairs cannot be rebuilt from the tree, which is the point of the proposal. Later announcements for review agents were closer but still high, by the session's report. Literature consulted: none.
