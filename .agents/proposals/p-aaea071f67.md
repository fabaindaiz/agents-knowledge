A proposal cannot be edited, since its id seals its content, so a privacy warning the owner answers on a proposal already written can only be answered by the carrier rewriting it: the old one deleted unreceived, a new one written generalised or carrying `privacy-allow: <reason>`, and the carrier gathered again. The harvest and close procedures should say so, and `propose` could offer the rewrite.

## Evidence

At the 0.0.29 intake the home's re-check held back four proposals of one carrier for quoted text; the owner reviewed each, all four were generalised, and the carrier rewrote them in a scratch worktree before a second gather took them in.
