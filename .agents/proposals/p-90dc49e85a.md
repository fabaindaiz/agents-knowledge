# Proposal: window-by-event-time

Windowing a stream by arrival time gives wrong results whenever data arrives late or out of order; what is computed, where in event time, when it is emitted and how refinements relate are separate choices, and batch, micro-batch and streaming then differ in latency and cost, not in meaning. Grouping by arrival, the usual answer, is wrong for any metric about when things happened. It stops applying where the result is meant to be about arrival, as in operational monitoring. The check: replay a shuffled copy of the input and compare with the in-order run.

## Evidence

Akidau et al., *The Dataflow Model* (VLDB 2015), its opening sections read.
