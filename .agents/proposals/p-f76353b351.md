This replaces the comprehension check in `p-b102eeae6f` and corrects its wording, after an adversarial review the same day. The check's wrong answers are the consequences of the other real options, never invented ones, plus *none of these*; a wrong pick or *none* reopens the decision rather than re-explaining it, since the agent writes the right answer and a misreading of its own would otherwise be confirmed. Before an irreversible decision, the agent's assumptions are shown unasked. The block limit counts prose only: each option's case, price and what it forecloses go in the option or a table, and are never cut to fit, since cutting them unevenly is the nudge §15 forbids. *Review more* goes on decision questions, not on the questions asked once at the start of a run or on fact questions; in a batch, a mode re-asks only that question and the ones that depend on it. Where `p-b102eeae6f` or d-5ed7e8-292961 still name a fixed *explain this more* answer, read the single *review more* entry of d-5ed7e8-0fec6e.

Decision d-5ed7e8-d0cfa3, by the owner, superseding d-5ed7e8-88e016.
