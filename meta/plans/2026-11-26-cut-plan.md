## 7. Drafts

**Step 4 → ~700** (card checks, gate floor, *Verification is a step* kept):

> **A document that tells a reader what to run is verified by running it**, command by command in a scratch copy;
> with no disposable target, a tested undo section and one approved real run. **The review in a fresh context runs
> only when asked**, or is offered with its cost when a multi-task plan ends; a change that deletes stored data,
> moves money or touches authentication offers one in one line (§21.4). **Before calling a red check yours,
> measure the untouched base** in a clean export of `HEAD`. **A change meant to change nothing is proved
> mechanically**: capture what it must preserve, compare byte for byte, normalising named nondeterminism; its own
> commit, stating the comparison.

**Step 8 → ~520** (closing question kept):

> **A. Re-run what this change invalidated**: a measurement, a settled question (a `d-` row, enforcer filled), an
> unblocked entry, a newly checkable rule, a research entry, a document not opened. **B. Route what the session
> knows that the repository does not** to its owning document; the general part becomes a proposal

> 17). **Capture is unconditional**: **no session is allowed to lose what it learned.** Propose only on a second
