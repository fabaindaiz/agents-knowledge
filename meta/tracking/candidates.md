# Candidates — learnings that have not passed admission

**A learning offered by a harvest that is not yet a note or a method rule.** It waits here
until it passes the five admission steps in `sources/README.md` (*The lifecycle of a note*), is
folded into an existing note, or is discarded with a reason. Nothing here is followed as
guidance — it is a queue, not a knowledge base. `.agents/knowledge/OPEN.md` shows it to every
carrier, without the evidence.

**One table, one row per candidate**: slug and claim, kind (**K** knowledge, **M** method), what
it lacks, where the evidence lives (by the kind of repository in neutral words, never by name or id),
when it was first offered, and *Since*, the first release tag whose queue holds it. The history
before `v0.0.20` is not tagged, so a *Since* of `0.0.20` means that release or earlier. `release.py intake`
adds the candidates the carriers' proposals offer; a slug already known goes to *Offered again, to merge*
at the end, and the release adds it to its row by hand; a refused row goes to `history.md`. Nothing
leaves this table by age: a row leaves only by a release's decision — admitted, folded, fixed, refused or
dropped — recorded in `history.md` with its reason. `INDEX.md`, generated by `release.py build`, lists
every slug of this table, of the history and of the notes under review or retired, so no meta-session
creates the same idea twice.
